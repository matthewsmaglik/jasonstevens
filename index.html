About

What I've been doing

I started in production in New York in 2008 and trained in film and photography at Montana State University. Since then I've worked in Montana, New York and, for the last eight years, Los Angeles as a member of IATSE Local 80.

I've shot an independent feature as director of photography and developed and produced my own projects. On studio films and series for directors including Steven Spielberg, Todd Phillips, Joel Coen and James Mangold, I've worked on the camera and lighting teams, and led crews of 10 to 12 on Joker: Folie à Deux and The Fabelmans.

The common thread is taking someone's vision and turning it into something a team can build, on time and to a high standard. Now based in Oregon, I want to bring that to one company and help develop and produce its work over the long term.
